# Figures

This directory contains the final figures accompanying the revised manuscript *“Is the world ready for small modular reactors? Longitudinal insights into market readiness”*.

## Main figures

Figures 1–10 present the longitudinal, temporal, technological and geographical analyses of the global SMR landscape and market readiness.

## Supplementary figures

Supplementary figures provide additional analyses and robustness assessments supporting the main manuscript.

## Reproducibility

The corresponding source-data workbooks are available in `revision/data/`. The R scripts used to generate the figures will be provided in `revision/scripts/`.

The original-submission figures remain available in the repository's top-level `outputs/` directory.
