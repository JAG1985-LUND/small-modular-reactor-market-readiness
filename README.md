# small-modular-reactor-market-readiness

Data, code and reproducible outputs for the study “Is the world ready for small modular reactors? Longitudinal insights into market readiness”, submitted to *Energy Strategy Reviews*.

This repository contains the curated source-data files, R scripts, figures and supplementary materials supporting the study. The analysis integrates successive International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) small modular reactor catalogues (2012–2026) and all four editions of the Nuclear Energy Agency (NEA) SMR Dashboard to examine reactor development, market readiness and their temporal, technological and geographical associations. An external assessment of development phases and technology readiness levels provides complementary evidence of differences in reactor maturity.

## Current manuscript and revised materials

The latest source data, analytical scripts, figures and supplementary tables are available in the [`revision/`](revision/) directory.

These materials supersede the original analysis and should be used when reproducing the results of the revised manuscript.

The revised materials are organised as follows:

- [`revision/data/`](revision/data/) – Source-data workbooks, Supplementary Tables S1–S18 and associated analytical outputs.
- [`revision/figures/`](revision/figures/) – Main Figures 1–10 and Supplementary Figures S1–S3, including panels S3(a) and S3(b).
- [`revision/scripts/`](revision/scripts/) – R scripts supporting the main figures, supplementary figures, statistical analyses and robustness checks.

Further information is provided in the [revision README](revision/README.md) and the README files within the respective directories.

## Original manuscript and preprint

The study was originally submitted under the title “From pipeline growth to first-of-a-kind deployment: market readiness across the global small modular reactor landscape”.

The original preprint was posted on SSRN on 17 August 2026 and remains available at:

https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7301832

The manuscript was subsequently revised to incorporate an expanded longitudinal analysis, the fourth edition of the NEA SMR Dashboard and a broader assessment of SMR market readiness.

The original preprint and associated analytical materials have been superseded by the revised manuscript and are retained for historical transparency.

## Repository structure

- `revision/` – Current manuscript source data, R scripts, figures and supplementary materials.
- `data/` – Original figure-specific source-data files and curated analytical datasets.
- `scripts/` – Original R scripts used to reproduce figures and table outputs.
- `outputs/` – Original exported figures, graphical abstract panel files and model-output summaries.

## Notes

The repository documents the reproducible data and code archive associated with the study. Working files, exploratory spreadsheets and undocumented intermediate files are not included.

Final journal-formatted figures, tables or supplementary files may differ slightly from these outputs if reformatted during submission or production.
